# Voltic Sparkling Energy: Global Sports Partnerships Review, 2015 to 2025

Internal memo, prepared for the 2026 planning offsite. Voltic is a fictional brand; this memo accompanies the sponsorship ledger export.

## Timeline of strategic decisions

- January 2015, Chicago: Voltic's first sports partnership budget was approved at headquarters, 4.2 million dollars, aimed at running and basketball events in the United States and the United Kingdom.
- March 2016, London: European headquarters opened in Shoreditch to manage football and motorsport deals.
- May 2017, Monaco: first motorsport title deal signed at the Monaco Grand Prix weekend, which doubled European spend that year.
- June 2019, Mumbai: India market entry began with sampling at cricket venues ahead of the September 2019 T20 league final title sponsorship. Indian sales reached 11 million dollars in the first twelve months.
- March 2020, global: the pandemic cancelled or postponed 31 contracted events between March and November 2020. Roughly 60 percent of fees were recovered through force majeure clauses.
- July 2021, Tokyo: hospitality commitments for the Summer Games went ahead without spectators; budget was redirected to retail sampling in Tokyo and Osaka.
- January 2022, Singapore: the Asia Pacific growth plan was approved, moving the regional hub from Sydney to Singapore and committing to cricket as the priority sport in India.
- November 2022, Doha: the World Cup fan festival activation, 12.4 million dollars, was the largest single commitment in company history and drove a 38 percent lift in Middle East sales in the following quarter.
- March 9, 2023, Sao Paulo: a voluntary recall of 1.2 million cans produced at the Jundiai plant was announced after a labeling error on caffeine content. Latin American sponsorships were suspended for the rest of 2023, including the Sao Paulo Supercup title deal.
- October 2023, Nairobi: an Africa pilot was approved with marathon partnerships in Nairobi and Lagos.
- February 2024, Los Angeles: the Big Game weekend fan experience marked the return of large North American activations after two years of reduced spend.
- June 2025, Lagos: first African title sponsorship, the Lagos Street Football Championship.
- July 2025, New York: Club World Cup fan fest activation, used as a dress rehearsal for the 2026 World Cup in North America.

## Open questions for 2026

1. Does sampling spend reliably precede title sponsorship success in new markets, as it appeared to in India?
2. Did the European pullback from 2022 onward cost share in Germany and Spain?
3. Was the Doha spend an outlier that should not anchor the 2026 North America budget?
