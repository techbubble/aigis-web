# Our Summer in Europe, 2025

Travel journal kept by Priya. Family of four: Priya, Dev, Maya (12) and Arjun (9). Four weeks, ten cities, two backpacks per person, one very patient nine year old.

## Saturday, June 21, 2025 - London

Landed at Heathrow at 7:40 in the morning after a sleepless overnight flight from San Francisco. Took the Elizabeth line into town and checked in early at a flat in Bloomsbury near Russell Square. Dev's suitcase did not arrive. British Airways says it is in Dublin. Walked to the British Museum in the afternoon to stay awake; Arjun fell asleep on a bench in front of the Rosetta Stone.

## Sunday, June 22, 2025 - London

Changing of the Guard at Buckingham Palace at 11:00, then a long walk through St James's Park and across Westminster Bridge. Rode the London Eye at 4 pm. Dinner at Dishoom in Covent Garden, 48 pounds for four. Suitcase delivered to the flat at 9:30 pm.

## Monday, June 23, 2025 - London

Day trip by train from King's Cross to Cambridge. Punting on the Cam, King's College Chapel. Maya has decided she will go to Cambridge. Back in London by 7.

## Tuesday, June 24, 2025 - London to Paris

Eurostar from St Pancras at 10:24, arrived Gare du Nord at 13:47. Apartment in Le Marais on Rue des Rosiers. First falafel of the trip at L'As du Fallafel. Evening walk along the Seine to Notre-Dame, reopened since December; the inside is bright in a way none of the photos prepared us for.

## Wednesday, June 25, 2025 - Paris

Louvre at 9 am with timed tickets. Two hours was the limit for the kids. Mona Lisa, Winged Victory, Egyptian wing. Lunch in the Tuileries. Climbed the Arc de Triomphe at 5 pm (284 steps, Arjun counted). The heat is building; 33 degrees today.

## Thursday, June 26, 2025 - Paris

Eiffel Tower summit at 10:30 (booked in March). Afternoon at Musée d'Orsay. Arjun started running a fever in the evening, 38.6. Pharmacy on Rue de Rivoli for paracetamol, 6.40 euros.

## Friday, June 27, 2025 - Paris

Arjun stayed in with Dev. Maya and I went to Versailles by RER C, Hall of Mirrors and the gardens, 4 hours. Temperature hit 36 degrees. Paris has issued a heat warning. Fever broke by dinner.

## Saturday, June 28, 2025 - Paris to Amsterdam

Thalys (now Eurostar) from Gare du Nord at 9:25 to Amsterdam Centraal, 12:45. Houseboat rental on the Prinsengracht. Canal cruise at 5 pm. Rented four bikes for three days, 90 euros.

## Sunday, June 29, 2025 - Amsterdam

Anne Frank House at 9:15 (tickets bought six weeks ago). Quiet kids for the rest of the morning. Vondelpark picnic, Rijksmuseum in the afternoon, the Night Watch is being restored in a glass box in the gallery. Dinner: stroopwafels count as dinner.

## Monday, June 30, 2025 - Amsterdam

Day trip by bike to Zaanse Schans, windmills and a clog workshop, about 20 km each way. Arjun's chain came off twice. Heat wave broke, 24 degrees and perfect.

## Tuesday, July 1, 2025 - Amsterdam to Berlin

Train from Amsterdam Centraal at 9:00 to Berlin Hauptbahnhof, arrived 15:25. Apartment in Prenzlauer Berg. Walked to the Brandenburg Gate and the Reichstag at dusk.

## Wednesday, July 2, 2025 - Berlin

Berlin Wall Memorial on Bernauer Strasse in the morning, then the East Side Gallery. Maya asked a lot of hard questions. Afternoon at the Pergamon's temporary exhibition (the main building is closed for renovation until 2027). Currywurst at Konnopke's, 18 euros for four.

## Thursday, July 3, 2025 - Berlin

Museum Island: Neues Museum for Nefertiti. Afternoon at Tempelhofer Feld, the old airport turned park; rented a go-kart for Arjun. Evening at a beer garden in Prater Garten.

## Friday, July 4, 2025 - Berlin to Prague

Train from Berlin Hbf at 8:30 along the Elbe, arrived Praha hlavni nadrazi at 12:50. Hotel near Old Town Square. Astronomical Clock at 3 pm, Charles Bridge at sunset. Trdelnik, twice.

## Saturday, July 5, 2025 - Prague

Prague Castle and St Vitus Cathedral in the morning, Golden Lane. Afternoon boat on the Vltava. Dev's phone was pickpocketed on tram 22 around 2 pm; reported at the police station on Jungmannovo namesti. Spent the evening changing passwords.

## Sunday, July 6, 2025 - Prague to Vienna

Railjet from Prague at 10:50 to Wien Hauptbahnhof, 14:55. Apartment in Neubau. Evening at the Naschmarkt.

## Monday, July 7, 2025 - Vienna

Schönbrunn Palace at 9 am, the maze and the Gloriette. Afternoon at the Kunsthistorisches Museum: Bruegel's Hunters in the Snow and Tower of Babel, Maya's favorite room of the trip so far. Sachertorte at Café Sacher, 29 euros for two slices and two hot chocolates. The heat is back, 34 degrees.

## Tuesday, July 8, 2025 - Vienna to Venice

Nightjet was sold out, so we flew Vienna to Venice Marco Polo at 7:10, landed 8:20. Vaporetto down the Grand Canal to an apartment in Cannaregio. St Mark's Basilica at 2 pm. Gelato count for the day: 3.

## Wednesday, July 9, 2025 - Venice

Doge's Palace and the Bridge of Sighs in the morning. Boat to Murano to watch glassblowing, then Burano for the painted houses. Maya bought a glass pendant, 35 euros. Acqua alta siren at 11 pm, water came up to the first step of our door.

## Thursday, July 10, 2025 - Venice to Florence

Frecciarossa from Venezia Santa Lucia at 9:25 to Firenze Santa Maria Novella, 11:40. Apartment in Oltrarno near Santo Spirito. Climbed Brunelleschi's dome at 4 pm, 463 steps, 38 degrees outside. Everyone was done by 6.

## Friday, July 11, 2025 - Florence

Uffizi at 8:15: Botticelli's Primavera and Birth of Venus, Leonardo's Annunciation. Lunch at the Mercato Centrale. Accademia at 4 pm for Michelangelo's David. Sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo.

## Saturday, July 12, 2025 - Florence

Day trip to Siena by bus, 75 minutes. Piazza del Campo, the Duomo's striped marble. Arjun tripped on the steps of the Torre del Mangia and needed a bandage, nothing serious. Back in Florence for Dev's birthday dinner at Trattoria Sostanza, 126 euros.

## Sunday, July 13, 2025 - Florence to Rome

Frecciarossa 10:00 to Roma Termini, 11:35. Apartment in Monti. Colosseum underground tour at 3 pm, then the Forum. 37 degrees; Rome has activated its heat emergency plan and the fountains are our best friends.

## Monday, July 14, 2025 - Rome

Vatican Museums at 8 am: Raphael Rooms, Sistine Chapel, St Peter's. Jubilee year crowds are intense. Afternoon rest, evening at Trastevere. Carbonara at Da Enzo, waited 50 minutes.

## Tuesday, July 15, 2025 - Rome

Borghese Gallery at 9 am (Bernini's Apollo and Daphne). Pantheon, Trevi Fountain, Spanish Steps. Gelato count: 4. Total gelato for the trip so far: 41, Arjun is keeping the tally.

## Wednesday, July 16, 2025 - Rome to Barcelona

Vueling from Fiumicino at 11:15 to Barcelona El Prat, 13:10. Apartment in Gràcia. Evening at the beach in Barceloneta, the kids' first swim in the Mediterranean.

## Thursday, July 17, 2025 - Barcelona

Sagrada Familia at 9 am, the towers are nearly finished. Park Güell at 1 pm. Tapas on Carrer de Blai, 54 euros. Maya is now fluent in ordering patatas bravas.

## Friday, July 18, 2025 - Barcelona

Day trip to Montserrat by train and rack railway. The boys' choir sang at 1 pm. Back in the city for a last dinner at La Boqueria. Packed. Arjun's final gelato count: 47.

## Saturday, July 19, 2025 - Barcelona to home

Flight from El Prat at 10:40 via London to San Francisco. Four weeks, ten cities, one lost suitcase, one fever, one stolen phone, one scraped knee, zero regrets.
