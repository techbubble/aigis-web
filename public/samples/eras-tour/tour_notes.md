# The Eras Tour: notes for the map

Compiled from public reporting on Taylor Swift's sixth concert tour. The show ledger (date, city, venue, per-show attendance, opening acts) is in shows.csv. These notes cover what happened around the shows.

## Announcement and ticketing

On November 1, 2022, during promotion for the album Midnights, Taylor Swift announced the Eras Tour on Good Morning America and on social media, describing it as a journey through the musical eras of her career. The promoter was Messina Touring Group, an AEG partner. The first announcement covered 27 US shows from March to August 2023, opening in Glendale, Arizona and closing in Inglewood, California. Demand led to eight more US dates on November 4 and seventeen more the following week.

On November 15, 2022, the first day of the US presale, Ticketmaster's website crashed under what Variety called historically unprecedented demand. Live Nation said it had prepared for 1.5 million verified fans and 14 million turned up. The general sale was cancelled. The failure became the subject of state, federal and congressional inquiries and an antitrust lawsuit against Live Nation, and inspired a string of anti-scalping and junk-fee proposals.

On June 2, 2023, the Latin American dates were announced, four nights in Mexico City in August and nine in Buenos Aires, Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo in November, with Sabrina Carpenter as the opening act. A third Buenos Aires show was added on June 6, two hours after the public sale began. The Asia-Pacific dates (Tokyo, Melbourne, Sydney, Singapore) were announced on June 20, 2023 and the European dates on June 21, 2023. A second North American leg for late 2024 was announced on August 4, 2023, with Gracie Abrams opening.

## The show

The set ran more than three and a half hours and over 40 songs, grouped into ten acts, one per album era. From May 9, 2024, in Paris, the show was rebuilt to add The Tortured Poets Department. Every night included two surprise songs not on the standard set list.

## Records and releases along the way

The Los Angeles run at SoFi Stadium in August 2023 was filmed for a concert film, Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour, released to cinemas worldwide on October 13, 2023, in a distribution deal that bypassed the major studios. It became the highest-grossing concert film in history.

During the tour Swift released Speak Now (Taylor's Version) on July 7, 2023, announced at the Nashville show on May 5, and 1989 (Taylor's Version) on October 27, 2023, announced at the final Los Angeles show on August 9. The Tortured Poets Department was announced at the Grammy Awards on February 4, 2024, and released April 19, 2024, between the Singapore and Paris legs.

In December 2023, after 57 shows, Swift's net worth was reported at 1.1 billion dollars, making her the first billionaire whose main source of income is music. The tour became the first to gross over one billion dollars, reported in December 2023 after the São Paulo shows, and then the first to pass two billion dollars, with the final total reported at just over two billion dollars after the last show in Vancouver on December 8, 2024.

## Incidents

Rio de Janeiro, November 17, 2023: the first show at Estádio Nilton Santos took place during a heat wave with a heat index of 59 degrees Celsius. Fans were prohibited from bringing water in and waited for hours in the sun. A 23 year old attendee, Ana Clara Benevides, collapsed during the opening songs and died of heat exhaustion. The show scheduled for November 18 was postponed to November 20 because of the extreme temperatures. A criminal investigation into the promoter, T4F, followed. Earlier, on June 11, 2023, armed scalpers had caused unrest outside the Brazilian box offices, and on June 20 Brazilian lawmakers filed the so-called Taylor Swift Law against scalping.

Buenos Aires, November 10, 2023: the second of three shows at Estadio River Plate was postponed to November 12 because of heavy rain and storms.

Singapore, March 2 to 9, 2024: the six shows at the National Stadium were the only ones in Southeast Asia. Thailand's prime minister said Singapore had paid a subsidy of two to three million dollars per show for exclusivity, and politicians in the Philippines and Indonesia complained. Singapore confirmed a grant but not its size.

Gelsenkirchen, July 18, 2024: a suspected stalker was arrested trying to enter the second show at Veltins-Arena.

Southport, July 29, 2024: three children were killed in a stabbing at a Taylor Swift themed dance class in Southport, England, which set off riots across the UK in the following days. Swift performed in London from August 15 and met survivors.

Vienna, August 8 to 10, 2024: all three shows at Ernst-Happel-Stadion were cancelled after Austrian authorities arrested three teenagers who had planned an ISIS-inspired attack intended to kill tens of thousands at the stadium. The Austrian broadcaster ORF aired the concert film for free on August 10. Security for the London shows at Wembley, August 15 to 20, was increased.

London, October 2024: a controversy over free tickets given to ministers of the UK government, and over the police escort provided for the Wembley shows, became a political scandal.

## Economics

Local reporting in nearly every city described sold-out hotels and higher spending in restaurants and shops in the days around the shows, a pattern reporters labelled Swiftonomics. The US Federal Reserve's Beige Book for July 2023 cited the Philadelphia shows as the strongest month for hotel revenue in the city since the pandemic. Glendale renamed itself Swift City for the opening weekend of March 17 and 18, 2023. Santa Clara made Swift honorary mayor for July 28 and 29, 2023. Rio projected a 'Welcome to Brazil' message on the Christ the Redeemer statue on November 16, 2023. Vancouver's final shows were estimated to bring in over 150 million Canadian dollars.

## Totals

149 shows in 51 cities across 21 countries on five continents, from Glendale on March 17, 2023 to Vancouver on December 8, 2024. Total attendance just over ten million. Six Guinness World Records and the iHeartRadio Music Award for Tour of the Century.
