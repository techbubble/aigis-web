# Sources

- Johns Hopkins University CSSE COVID-19 Data Repository, time_series_covid19_confirmed_global.csv and time_series_covid19_deaths_global.csv (github.com/CSSEGISandData/COVID-19, archived March 2023). Used for first_cases_and_deaths_by_region.csv and weekly_cases_top30_countries.xlsx; coordinates are the repository's.
- World Health Organization, Novel Coronavirus (2019-nCoV) Situation Reports 1 to 92, 21 January to 21 April 2020 (who.int/emergencies/diseases/novel-coronavirus-2019/situation-reports). Used for who_situation_digest.pdf.
- WHO, Timeline: WHO's COVID-19 response (who.int/emergencies/diseases/novel-coronavirus-2019/interactive-timeline).
- Wikipedia, "Timeline of the COVID-19 pandemic in January 2020" through "April 2020", and "COVID-19 lockdowns". Used for lockdowns_and_declarations.md.
- National government announcements as reported by Reuters, BBC and AP for lockdown dates and population figures.
