# COVID-19: the first 90 days

Demo sources for AIGIS: the spread of COVID-19 from a market in Wuhan to a pandemic, 31 December 2019 to 21 April 2020. Drop any or all of these into Studio.

- first_cases_and_deaths_by_region.csv: 467 rows, the date each country or province reported its first confirmed case and its first death, with coordinates and the cumulative count reached by 21 April 2020 as the value. Plays back as the spread across the map.
- weekly_cases_top30_countries.xlsx: 420 rows, weekly cumulative and new cases and deaths for the 30 most affected countries, so marker sizes grow week by week.
- who_situation_digest.pdf: a narrative digest of the WHO situation reports and national announcements, dated and placed.
- lockdowns_and_declarations.md: 36 lockdowns, emergency declarations and WHO decisions with place, date and people affected.
- SOURCES.md: where the facts came from.

Suggested questions once the story is built: "Replay the spread from Wuhan", "Which countries reported a first death within two weeks of their first case?", "Where were the largest lockdowns, and in what order?", "When did Europe overtake China?"
