# Meta events worldwide

Demo sources for AIGIS: Meta Platforms from a Harvard dorm in 2004 to gigawatt data centers in 2026. Drop any or all of these into Studio.

- meta_timeline_2004_2026.csv: 58 dated, located company events (acquisitions, launches, fines, outages, layoffs, infrastructure) with values where they exist.
- meta_data_centers.xlsx: every announced data center with site, date, investment and capacity.
- connect_and_launches.pdf: a narrative of the Connect conferences and hardware and model launches, 2014 to 2026.
- regulatory_actions.md: fines, settlements and rulings by jurisdiction, 2018 to 2026.
- SOURCES.md: where the facts came from.

Suggested questions once the story is built: "Replay the data center buildout", "Where has Meta been fined, and for how much?", "What happened in the month after Superintelligence Labs was formed?", "Which events carry the largest dollar values?"
